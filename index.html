A notebook that does the arithmetic

Write it like a note

One food per line, the way you'd say it: "half a chicken burrito", "big oat latte". Meal words become headers with subtotals. Paste a whole list, or dictate it.

Fast, and it learns

Common foods come from a nutrition database on your iPhone, built from USDA FoodData Central, so most lines resolve right away, even offline. Lines you've written before come back with your own numbers.

Every number explains itself

Tap a value to see the foods, the grams, the source and how sure Morsel is. Drag the grams, switch off an assumption, or fix it in words: "only half the rice".

Undo anything

Every line is its own entry, so nothing overwrites anything. Undo and redo everywhere, and Recently Deleted keeps lines for 30 days.

Barcode scanning, free

Scan a package and pick the servings. Product data comes from Open Food Facts. Unknown product? Photograph the label instead.

Goals you can see the math for

Your daily target shows its formula and your chosen rate. Track protein closely, or turn on Gentle mode to hide calories and just keep a journal.

Apple Health, widgets and Siri

Save nutrition and water to Apple Health. See what's left on your Home Screen and Lock Screen, add water from a widget, and log with "Log food in Morsel".

Morsel Pro, when you want more

Snap a meal and get words you can edit, Think harder on tricky lines, Insights from your own data, Fill my macros, recipes and more.

Honest about what it needs. Smart parsing uses Apple Intelligence on your iPhone. Without it, you can still search and pick foods, and common foods still resolve from the built-in database. Snap, Think harder and deep estimates for restaurant dishes need iOS 27 with Apple Intelligence.
